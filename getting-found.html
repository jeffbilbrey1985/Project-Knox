The work, plainly

So what does Knox actually do? Four things, every week.

No jargon, no mystery. Here is exactly what we build and keep running so your shop makes the short list.

01

Your box on the map, claimed and kept current.

When somebody Googles your trade, the first thing they see isn’t a website — it’s the boxes on the map. That box is your Google Business Profile. Most shops have never claimed theirs, or filled it out once and let it go stale.

We claim it, complete every part of it — hours, photos, services, posts — and keep it current. New photos when you have them, holiday hours before the holiday, not after.

Why it matters: the map box is the first thing Google shows — and Google trusts a profile that’s complete and looked after.

02

A site Google can actually read.

Fast, built for phones first, with your town and your services written the way your neighbors really search — “brake repair in Glen Ellyn,” not “automotive solutions.”

Behind the scenes, structured data spells out for Google exactly what you do, where you are, and when you’re open — so it never has to guess.

Why it matters: a slow site, or one Google can’t read, might as well be a closed door. Ours load fast and say plainly what you do.

03

Reviews that keep arriving.

After each visit, your customer gets one text asking for a review. That’s it — one text, same request to everyone, no pestering. Happy customers mean to leave a review; the text is what turns “meaning to” into done.

Then each review gets a reply from the owner — with the service and the town in it. A steady flow of fresh reviews and answered reviews shows Google, and your neighbors, that the business is alive.

Why it matters: 97% of people read reviews before choosing a local business — and Google itself says more reviews and positive ratings can help your local ranking.

Sources: BrightLocal — Local Consumer Review Survey · Google Business Profile Help

04

Everything reports back to one place.

This is the part that makes it Knox. The site, the booking, the reminders, the review requests — they’re not five tools with five logins. They’re one system, and it reports back to you.

A customer books — it’s on your calendar. A review lands — you see it. Somebody hasn’t been back in a while — the follow‑up goes out without you lifting a finger.

Why it matters: the shops that stay on the short list are the ones where this work happens every week. With Knox, it happens whether you’re busy or not.

One honest thing before you go: nobody can promise you the #1 spot on Google — Google doesn’t sell placement in local results, and anyone who guarantees it is telling you something. What we promise is the work: the profile, the site, the reviews, done every week and kept in writing.
